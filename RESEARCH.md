# Research notes

The first release uses a source-led approach: product format, weight and purity are shown, while prices, ratings, stock claims and partnership claims are withheld until a licensed feed is connected.

## Findings

- The Royal Mint groups physical bullion by bars, coins and weight, pairs the catalogue with education, and explains delivery and storage.
- GOLD.DE makes dealer transparency and comparable product categories central to its comparison flow.
- APMEX uses a broad product taxonomy spanning mints, series, weights and formats.
- BullionByPost supports weight-led browsing and clear product-family navigation.
